ABOUT EDUPREDICT

Empowering Every Learner Before It's Too Late

In conventional university portals, student underperformance is typically identified only after final semester examinations—leaving zero time for remediation.

EduPredict replaces retroactive grade-posting with proactive predictive guidance. By unifying students, faculty, and parents on a shared platform, we ensure vulnerable students receive targeted tutoring and encouragement weeks before examination halls open.
